Privacy Policy — MemoryMap

Home Privacy Terms

Last updated: 4 October 2026

MemoryMap ("the App") is a digital-heritage project for mapping necropolises and digitizing graves. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and what rights you have.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data (Firebase Authentication): e-mail address, authentication identifiers from Google or Apple sign-in, e-mail verification status.
  • Profile data (Supabase): display name, username, phone number and bio that you enter yourself.
  • User content: grave records you digitize (names, dates, epitaphs, cemetery, photos) and their moderation status.
  • Location: GPS coordinates only when you explicitly tap "Get GPS" or pick a point on the map while digitizing a grave. We never track your location in the background.
  • Notifications: Firebase Cloud Messaging token and your alert preference, used to inform you about moderation and OCR results.
  • Diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics, Analytics, Performance): anonymized crash reports and aggregated usage statistics that help us fix bugs.

We do not collect data about living persons intentionally. Do not upload photos or texts containing personal data of living people.

2. How we use data

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To store and display digitized graves and cemeteries.
  • To moderate content and run OCR on headstone photos.
  • To send moderation and OCR notifications (if enabled).
  • To ensure security, prevent abuse and improve the App.

Legal basis (GDPR, where applicable): performance of the service you requested, your consent (location, notifications), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and functional.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is processed by our infrastructure providers acting on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, push, crash reports, analytics).
  • Supabase (database and file storage for profiles, graves and photos).

Grave records you publish are visible to other users of the App by design — that is the purpose of the heritage map.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Account and profile data is kept while your account exists.
  • You can delete your account at any time: Profile → Delete account. Deletion removes your profile and unlinks your content from your identity; already published anonymized grave records may remain as part of the public heritage map.
  • You can ask us to erase your personal data by writing to grebenyukupp@gmail.com.

5. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR in the EU/EEA and Ukrainian data-protection law) you may request access, correction, export, restriction or erasure of your personal data, and withdraw consent at any time (e.g. by disabling notifications or location for the App).

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Do not register an account if you are below that age.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.

8. Contact

Data controller contact: grebenyukupp@gmail.com.
